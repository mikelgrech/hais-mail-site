Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026. This policy covers HAIS Mail, the mail reader in hOS.

What HAIS Mail accesses

When you connect a Gmail account, HAIS Mail asks Google for read-only access to that mailbox (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). It reads message headers, bodies, attachments and labels so it can show and search your mail. It never asks for permission to send, delete or modify mail.

Where your data goes

  • Mail is copied only into your own HAIS system, on computers you own and run. There is no HAIS Mail cloud service.
  • Google's access token is stored encrypted on your own HAIS server and is never shared.
  • Your data is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Your data is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

HAIS Mail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect an account at any time in HAIS Mail: Settings → Mail → Disconnect. HAIS then forgets its Google access.
  • You can also remove access from your Google Account: Security → Third-party apps & services → HAIS Mail.
  • Mail already imported stays on your own system until you delete it there.

Contact

Questions go to the support contact shown on the Google sign-in screen for HAIS Mail.
