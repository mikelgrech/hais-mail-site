● HAIS Mail

HAIS Mail is the mail reader in hOS, a personal desktop and home system. It brings a person's own Gmail into their own HAIS archive so they can read and search it alongside the rest of their work.

What it does

  • Reads Gmail read-only (the gmail.readonly permission). It cannot send, delete, label or change anything in Gmail.
  • Imports recent mail into the person's own HAIS system, which they run on their own computers.
  • Can be connected to more than one of the person's Gmail accounts, each with its own Google sign-in.

Who it is for

HAIS Mail is private software used by its owner and their household. It is not offered to the public.
